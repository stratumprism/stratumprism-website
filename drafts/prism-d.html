    (05) How we use AI

    Your data stays yours. Judgement stays human.

    AI makes us faster. It doesn’t make the decisions. These are the rules we work by.

    01

    A person signs off every recommendation.

    AI drafts the analysis. A partner checks it, challenges it, and puts their name to it.

    02

    Your data works only for you.

    We use your information for your engagement and nothing else, and we tell you which tools touch it.

    03

    You own everything we build.

    Dashboards, models and automations are yours, with the documentation to run them without us.

    04

    We’ll tell you when AI isn’t the answer.

    Sometimes the fix is a conversation, a role or a rule. We’ll say so.
